Layer 7 · MegaMind HQ

A sovereign AI brain, built from scratch.

2026 – nowin active development

MegaMind is an AI system I am building without relying on external AI models or APIs, to own the intelligence stack from the first neuron upwards. Today it drives a small robot: specialised neural networks for sight, hearing, language, movement and smell feed one shared core that decides what to do.

The robot learns in a digital twin, a virtual building seen through a model of its real camera, before it ever moves in the physical world.

Reasoning coreMulti-sense brain, trained in a digital twinReal worldMany robots, one brain
  • SeeRecognise what is ahead, people, faces and hand gestures from a low-cost camera
  • UnderstandFollow commands in English, French and Arabic with its own small language model
  • ExploreDrive on its own and build a map of where it has been
  • Stay safeKnow when it is unsure, and stay within safety rules that no learning can change
  • Learn togetherMany robots improve one shared brain without sharing raw data
  • Run smallFit on a microcontroller the size of a coin once compressed
